# Research Topics {#mpkb:section:sec-0052}

This section brings together scientific and engineering work relevant to molecular programming.
Topics range from foundational models to physical systems, experimental methods, and applications.

The topic areas are broad and connected. A subject may appear across more than one area, and the
categories provide a guide to the collection rather than a strict classification.

For cross-cutting relationships, see the curated landing pages and the **Related Topics** sections
within articles. Controlled topic tags support filtering, but they do not replace explicit links or
the folder hierarchy.

## Theoretical Foundations {#mpkb:section:sec-0053}

Models, concepts, and methods used to describe, analyze, and reason about molecular systems. This
includes computational models, algorithms, physical principles, and methods for treating uncertainty
and chemical dynamics.

## Structures and Dynamics {#mpkb:section:sec-1000}
The structures, interactions, assembly, motion, and behavior of molecular systems. Assembly
approaches connect to theoretical models, chemistry, and physical interfaces.

## Interfaces {#mpkb:section:sec-1001}
Methods and components that connect molecular systems to their environments, instruments, materials,
and experimental settings. Interfaces also provide synthesis, control, sensing, and characterization
methods used by structures, circuits, and integrated systems.

## Circuits {#mpkb:section:sec-1002}
Information processing and computation implemented through molecular and biochemical systems.
Circuit behavior connects to reaction-network models, stochastic effects, and system-level control.

## System Integration and Control {#mpkb:section:sec-1003}
The coordination of molecular components, processes, and control methods into functioning systems
and applications. This area connects circuits and interfaces with biological or chemical processes,
measurement, feedback, and application requirements.
