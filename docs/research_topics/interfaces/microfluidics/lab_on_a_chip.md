Focus: Integrating optical, fluorescence, or electrochemical sensors directly into the microfluidic
architecture.

Initial Content: The output layer of the lab-on-a-chip. Uses laser-induced fluorescence (LIF)
detection, high-speed imaging, or spectroscopic sensors to monitor reaction kinetics, read out
fluorescent reporter signals from DNA circuits, or sort droplets at high frequencies based on
real-time feedback.
