Focus: Generating picoliter-to-nanoliter water-in-oil droplets to serve as isolated reaction vessels.

Initial Content: Essential for high-throughput screening and in vitro compartmentalization (IVC). By
encapsulating individual synthetic gene circuits, enzymes, or DNA libraries into monodisperse
droplets, researchers prevent cross-talk and maintain genotype-phenotype linkage during directed
evolution or dynamic circuit characterization.
