Focus: Using multilayer soft lithography, pneumatic micro-valves, and programmable pumps for
automated fluid handling.

Initial Content: Ideal for complex, multi-step reaction networks and DNA circuit assembly. Pneumatic
valves enable precise routing, mixing, metering, and incubation of small-volume reagents without
chaotic turbulence, mimicking automated macroscopic lab benches at the micro-scale.
