Focus: Harnessing silicon photonic circuits, optoelectronic chips, and microscopic waveguides to generate, route, split, and detect light with immense bandwidth and near-zero latency.

Role in Molecular Programming: Unlike analytical microfluidic sensors that passively read downstream
signals, integrated photonics acts as an active optical control and computing architecture. By
delivering high-speed, parallel-addressed optical feedback loops and complex interference patterns
directly to light-responsive molecular arrays, photonic chips serve as hardware controllers that
drive precise spatial-temporal biochemical reactions in hybrid opto-chemical systems.
