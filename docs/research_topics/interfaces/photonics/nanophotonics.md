Nanophotonics investigates how light behaves on the nanometer scale and how it interacts with
nanostructures that are smaller than or comparable to the wavelength of light. The primary focus is
overcoming the traditional diffraction limit of light to confine, guide, and manipulate photons
using engineered sub-wavelength structures. Key examples include plasmonic metallic nanoparticles,
photonic crystals, metamaterials, and quantum dots, which enable nanoscale optical routing, enhanced
light-matter interactions, and advanced sensing or energy conversion.
