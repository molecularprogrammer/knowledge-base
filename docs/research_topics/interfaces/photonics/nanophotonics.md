Nanophotonics investigates how light behaves on the nanometer scale and how it interacts with nanostructures that are smaller than or comparable to the wavelength of light. The primary focus is overcoming the traditional diffraction limit of light to confine, guide, and manipulate photons using engineered sub-wavelength structures. Key examples include plasmonic metallic nanoparticles, photonic crystals, metamaterials, and quantum dots, which enable nanoscale optical routing, enhanced light-matter interactions, and advanced sensing or energy conversion.
