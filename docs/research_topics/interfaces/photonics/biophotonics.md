Biophotonics explores the interaction between light and biological matter, ranging from individual
molecules and cells up to complex tissues. The primary focus is developing optical technologies for
diagnostic, therapeutic, and analytical applications in medicine and biology. Common examples
include fluorescence microscopy, optical coherence tomography (OCT), laser surgery, and biosensors
used to detect disease biomarkers by tracking how light is absorbed, scattered, or emitted by
biological systems.
