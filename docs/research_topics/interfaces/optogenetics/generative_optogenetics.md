## Open Questions {#mpkb:section:sec-1049}

- Could light-responsive biological systems support targeted synthesis or molecular
  self-assembly? This remains a research question, not an established template-free DNA-printing
  capability.
- Which optical, mechanical, or scanning-probe methods could measure or manipulate molecular
  systems, and what are their scale, throughput, and cost limits?
- What existing cell lines and biological production systems can be used responsibly, and what
  safety, consent, and containment requirements apply?

Don’t wait for DARPA or big money; look at Wright bothers vs other teams; look at what can be done
quickly and at as low of a cost as possible; avoid Dogma about the “right” approach; potentially add
peripheral things that make adjustments, or rely totally on biology; avoid reliance on models that
may not be 100% true and applicable to the situation; eliminate bureaucracy; rapid feedback loop
between failure and next iteration
