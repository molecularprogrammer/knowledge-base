# Interfaces and Experimental Methods {#mpkb:section:sec-1021}

Interfaces connect molecular systems to their environment, experimental equipment, and applications.
This area includes synthesis and fabrication, sensing and characterization, optical and fluidic
methods, and interactions with surfaces and materials.

## Build and Manipulate {#mpkb:section:sec-1022}

Methods for producing, positioning, and controlling molecular components include chemical and
enzymatic synthesis, optical methods, microfluidics, and optogenetic control.
