Primary Objective	High-throughput fabrication and manufacturing of chemical or molecular libraries
(in vitro).

Operating Environment	Cell-free, in vitro chemical reaction platforms on solid surfaces
(chips/microarrays).

Core Mechanism	Photolabile chemical deprotection (using light to remove blocking groups and allow
stepwise nucleotide coupling).

Role in Molecular Programming	Acts as a fabrication engine to build complex DNA, RNA, or peptide
pools from digital designs.
