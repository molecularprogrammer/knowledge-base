Laser-driven microarray selection is an in vitro molecular engineering and high-throughput screening
tool. It uses lasers or optical projection systems to drive precise chemical reactions—such as
photolabile deprotection—to synthesize and screen massive pools of abiotic nucleic acids on a solid
support.
