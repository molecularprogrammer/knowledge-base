# Structures and Dynamics {#mpkb:section:sec-1018}

This area covers the physical organization and behavior of molecular systems, from designed
structures to dynamic machines. Related work addresses how components assemble, move, respond to
conditions, and maintain function.

## Molecular Structure and Assembly {#mpkb:section:sec-1019}

Topics include self-assembly, nucleic-acid structures, supramolecular organization, and templated
construction. DNA origami and other DNA nanostructures are related approaches, with different design
and assembly strategies.
