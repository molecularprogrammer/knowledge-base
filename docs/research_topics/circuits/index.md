# Molecular Circuits {#mpkb:section:sec-1015}

Molecular circuits process information through interactions among nucleic acids, proteins, and other
molecular components. Research spans circuit design, implementation, analysis, and connection to
larger systems.

## Circuit Design and Computation {#mpkb:section:sec-1016}

Circuit design describes how molecular components represent and transform signals. Topics include
logic gates, strand-displacement networks, circuit composition, and computational behavior.

## Chemical and Biological Implementations {#mpkb:section:sec-1017}

Molecular circuits can be implemented as chemical reaction networks or engineered biological
systems. Their behavior depends on reaction rates, noise, resource limits, and interactions with the
host system. Related foundations include [reaction-network models](../theoretical_foundations/chemical_reaction_networks/stochastic_modeling.md),
[reaction-diffusion systems](../theoretical_foundations/chemical_reaction_networks/reaction_diffusion_systems.md),
and [molecular noise](../theoretical_foundations/molecular_noise.md).

See [engineering biological circuits](../system_integration_and_control/engineering_biological_circuits.md)
for system-level implementation and integration topics.
