Focus: Harnessing the intrinsic spin of electrons within organic molecular architectures for ultra-low-power logic, non-volatile data storage, and spin-based computing.

Initial Content: Carbon-based organic molecules and polymers are uniquely suited for spin circuits because light elements (carbon and hydrogen) exhibit weak spin-orbit coupling and minimal hyperfine interactions. This allows electron spin states to be preserved for extended periods (typically microseconds) without premature relaxation. Researchers explore these systems to build organic magnetoresistive switches (OMAR) and spin-qubits. Furthermore, molecular spintronics can theoretically serve as a high-speed transducer, bridging slow wetware logic (such as DNA strand displacement circuits) with rapid, non-volatile electronic state readouts.
